## 1. The four conditions

| Condition | Device | Light | How to set it up |
|---|---|---|---|
| `laptop_normal` | Laptop with built-in webcam | **300–500 lux** at the face | Ordinary room lighting, lights on, no window directly behind the volunteer |
| `laptop_dim` | Same laptop | **below 50 lux** | Main lights off, one lamp or only the screen light |
| `phone_normal` | Volunteer's own phone (or the team's test phone) | 300–500 lux | As above |
| `phone_dim` | Same phone | below 50 lux | As above |

- Measure the light at the volunteer's face with a phone light-meter app before each block and write it in
  the session sheet (`attack-protocol.md` §4). Repeat if the value is outside the range.
- Record the device and browser (e.g. "Lenovo laptop, Chrome 131"; "Samsung A54, Chrome 131").
- The **condition** is stored automatically in every attempt row (`device_type` + `light`/`light_hint`). The
  researcher checks that it matches the planned condition.

## 2. Which factor is tested where

Some factors cannot run in every condition (their `is_available()` rules, `docs/interface.md`). Those cells
are **not tested**; they count as "not available" in the analysis.

| Factor | `laptop_normal` | `laptop_dim` | `phone_normal` | `phone_dim` | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| password | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | |
| passkey | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Needs WebAuthn (Windows Hello / phone fingerprint or PIN) |
| face | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | **The factor most affected by light** |
| keystroke | ✔ | ✔ | — | — | Phone keyboards give unreliable timings (keystroke DESIGN §6) |
| phone_approval | ✔ | ✔ | — | — | The QR must be scanned with a second device |

**16 tested cells.**

## 3. Trials per volunteer

Light matters mainly for face, so face gets full trials in every condition; the other factors get full
trials in normal light and fewer in dim light (to check light really makes no difference).

**Genuine trials** (the volunteer logs in to their own account):

| Factor | laptop_normal | laptop_dim | phone_normal | phone_dim | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| password | 10 | 5 | 10 | 5 | 30 |
| passkey | 10 | 5 | 10 | 5 | 30 |
| face | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 40 |
| keystroke | 10 | 5 | — | — | 15 |
| phone_approval | 10 | 5 | — | — | 15 |
| **Total** | | | | | **130** |

Keystroke trials happen during password trials on the laptop (same typing), so they add no extra time.

**Imposter trials** (the volunteer tries another volunteer's account; rotation in `attack-protocol.md` §2):

| Factor | Per victim | Victims | Conditions | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| face | 5 | 2 | 4 | 40 |
| keystroke (`imposter_typing`) | 5 | 2 | 2 | 20 |

**Attack trials** (printed photo, QR relay, …) are run by the team on at least 3 victims each, following
the cards in `attack-protocol.md`, not by every volunteer.

## 4. How much data this gives

With **12 volunteers** (target 10–15):

| Cell | Genuine | Imposter | Enough for (metrics.md §4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| face × any condition | 120 | 120 | EER (≥ 50 + 50) ✔ |
| keystroke × laptop_normal / laptop_dim | 120 / 60 | 120 | EER ✔ |
| password, passkey × normal | 120 | — | FRR, genuine success ✔ |
| any factor × dim (not face) | 60 | — | genuine success; compare with normal |

Large datasets (LFW, CMU) give the main accuracy numbers; volunteer data checks whether they hold on our
real system and conditions (`docs/decisions.md` D2).

## 5. Session plan

| Session | Device | Order | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Laptop | Consent → enrollment (all factors, normal light) → block 1 → block 2 → imposter block | ~45 min |
| **B** | Phone (another day if possible) | Enrollment on the phone (passkey, face) → block 1 → block 2 → imposter block → SUS questionnaire | ~30 min |

- **Counterbalancing:** odd volunteer codes do **normal light first**, even codes do **dim light first**, so
  tiredness or learning does not all fall on one condition.
- Inside a block, the order of factors is shuffled (random list printed on the session sheet).
- Before every block, the researcher sets the study label in the admin dashboard
  (`PUT /admin/study-label`: genuine, imposter, or attack code + session id; `docs/interface.md` §7).
- 2-minute break between blocks.
